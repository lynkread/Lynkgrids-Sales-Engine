# Slop patterns — run on every message

AI-tell catalogue. If a note trips two or more, rewrite.

## Instant fails

- "I hope this message finds you well"
- "Just circling back" as the whole message
- "Unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "elevate your"
- "As a [title] at [company], you understand the importance of"
- Compliment with no object: "Great insights lately"
- Invented personalisation: "Loved your recent post on leadership"
- Paragraph walls on LinkedIn
- Three questions in one note
- "Quick question" followed by a paragraph that is not a question

## Soft fails (rewrite)

- Starting every note with "Noticed that…"
- Restating their About section back to them
- Our product name in the connection request
- "Would love to hop on a call" with no reason
- Em dashes stacked, emoji stacks, fake casual "hey hey"

## Pass test

Read it out loud. If you would be embarrassed to send it from your own account to a peer, it fails. Specific, short, adult.
